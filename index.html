Strategy reference

The setup: pullback in trend

  1. Trend context — price consistently above (buy) or below (sell) the 50-period EMA on the H4 chart, with clear higher-highs/higher-lows or lower-highs/lower-lows.
  2. Pullback — price retraces toward the EMA or a recent swing level, without breaking trend structure.
  3. Confirmation candle — a decisive reversal candle at the pullback zone, in the direction of the trend.
  4. Stop-loss — placed just beyond the swing high/low that defines the pullback.
  5. Target — 2–3x the stop distance, checked against nearby resistance/support for room to run.
  6. Position size — calculated backward from stop distance so risk is capped at 100 KES.

If any one of these six isn't met, it's not a valid setup — sitting out is a correct outcome of the system, not a failure of it.
